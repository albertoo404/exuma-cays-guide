Welcome to the Exuma Cays

Discover Staniel Cay

Staniel Cay is a popular base for exploring the central Exuma Cays. Surrounded by clear water, small islands, reefs and white-sand beaches, it offers access to some of the region’s most memorable experiences.

From visiting the famous swimming pigs at Big Major Cay to snorkeling at Thunderball Grotto and seeing the nurse sharks at Compass Cay, the surrounding islands offer plenty to discover. Boat trips make it possible to experience several highlights in a single day, depending on weather and sea conditions.

Island Paradise

White-Sand Beaches

Discover peaceful stretches of white sand where you can unwind, swim and enjoy the clear Bahamian water. Some beaches around the cays are most conveniently reached by boat.

TURQUOISE WATERS

The waters surrounding Staniel Cay are known for their clarity and vivid shades of blue. The views are spectacular from shore and while traveling between islands.

Beneath the surface

A World Beneath the Water

The clear waters around Staniel Cay offer opportunities for snorkeling and swimming among tropical fish and fascinating underwater landscapes. Choose suitable locations, follow local guidance and respect marine life.

Make the most of your visit

Things to Do Around Staniel Cay

Choose experiences that suit your interests, schedule and sea conditions.

Snorkeling

Explore suitable reefs and underwater habitats, keeping a respectful distance from wildlife and coral.

Pig Beach

Visit Big Major Cay to see the well-known swimming pigs. Follow local guidance and do not feed wildlife unless specifically permitted.

Nurse Shark Encounter

Compass Cay is known for nurse shark encounters. Follow site rules and instructions from local guides.

Beach Day

Spend time on a quiet beach, swim where conditions are safe and enjoy the spectacular turquoise water.

Boat Adventure

Explore several cays and attractions on an island-hopping trip. Check weather, tides and access requirements before departure.

Fishing

Fishing excursions may be available through local operators. Check applicable regulations and arrange your trip with a qualified provider.
