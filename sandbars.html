Discover Exuma

Explore the Beautiful Exuma Sandbars

Exuma is renowned for its luminous white sandbars, shallow turquoise water and peaceful island scenery. As the tide falls, stretches of sand emerge from the sea, creating remarkable places to pause, swim, stroll and take in the view.

01 · A classic stop

Pipe Creek Sandbar

Enjoy soft white sand surrounded by clear turquoise water. Pipe Creek is a beautiful place to pause during a boat excursion, take a refreshing swim when conditions allow and appreciate the peaceful scenery of the Exuma Cays.

02 · Shallow-water escape

Copper Jack Cay Sandbar

Step into shallow, crystal-clear water and explore the bright white sand. With its beautiful natural colours and open island views, a sandbar stop offers a memorable setting for relaxing and capturing photographs in the Bahamian sun.

03 · Open horizons

Exuma Shallow Sand Banks

At low tide, wide stretches of pale sand can emerge from the sea. The contrast between the white seabed and surrounding turquoise water creates a spectacular natural backdrop for gentle exploration and photography.

04 · A quieter retreat

Hidden Sandbar Escape

Discover the quieter side of the Exuma waters. Depending on weather, tide and access, a boat excursion may offer the chance to stop at a secluded sandbar, enjoy the view and savour a peaceful moment away from busier beaches.

05 · Island paradise

Exuma Sandbar Paradise

Experience the celebrated combination of white sand and turquoise Bahamian water. These scenic stops can be a wonderful addition to an island outing for couples, families and groups exploring the Exuma Cays together.

Make the Most of Your Sandbar Experience

Sandbars are shaped by the tides, so the landscape can look different from one part of the day to another. A little preparation helps you enjoy the scenery comfortably and responsibly.

  • Check tide, weather and sea conditions before setting out.
  • Bring sun protection, drinking water and a camera in a waterproof bag.
  • Wear suitable footwear if walking over shell or coral fragments.
  • Respect marine life and leave the sandbar as you found it.
