Explore the Beautiful Exuma Sandbars

Exuma is famous for its brilliant white sandbars, shallow turquoise water and peaceful island scenery. These temporary beaches appear as the tide falls and create some of the most beautiful places to swim, walk and take photographs.

Pipe Creek Sandbar

Enjoy soft white sand surrounded by clear turquoise water. Pipe Creek is a beautiful place to stop during a boat excursion, swim and enjoy the peaceful Exuma scenery.

Copper Jack Cay Sandbar

Step into shallow crystal-clear water and explore the bright white sand. This type of sandbar is ideal for relaxing, taking photographs and enjoying the Caribbean sun.

Exuma Shallow Sand Banks

Wide stretches of white sand emerge from the sea at low tide. The surrounding turquoise water creates an incredible natural swimming and photography experience.

Hidden Sandbar Escape

Escape to a quieter part of the Exuma waters. Boat tours can stop at secluded sandbars where visitors can swim, relax and enjoy the scenery away from busy beaches.

Exuma Sandbar Paradise

Experience the famous combination of white sand and turquoise Caribbean water. It is a wonderful stop for couples, families and groups exploring the Exuma Cays.
