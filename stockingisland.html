Welcome to Stocking Island

A beautiful natural retreat just across Elizabeth Harbour from George Town, known for its sandy shoreline, clear water and relaxed Bahamian character.

Natural beauty, island character

Stocking Island offers a different pace from the busy parts of Exuma. Visitors can enjoy open views across the harbour, walk along the beach, explore the island’s natural landscape and spend time in the water.

The island is also known for Chat ’N’ Chill, a popular beachside gathering place where visitors can enjoy food, refreshments and the easy-going atmosphere of island life. Access is commonly by boat from the George Town area, so plan your crossing and return journey in advance.

Pristine Beaches

Swimming & Water Views

🍽️

Chat ’N’ Chill

Visit the well-known beach bar and restaurant for a relaxed stop by the water. Check local opening times before travelling.

🐚

Local Island Life

Take your time, appreciate the natural surroundings and experience the unhurried rhythm of this much-loved Exuma destination.

🚤

Boat Access

Stocking Island is commonly reached by water from George Town. Arrange a suitable boat or local transfer and confirm return plans.

Travel thoughtfully

Bring drinking water, sun protection and any essentials you may need. Check weather and sea conditions before travelling, respect wildlife and take your rubbish with you to help keep the island beautiful.
