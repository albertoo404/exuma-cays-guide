The natural side of Exuma

Explore the Exuma Cays Land & Sea Park

The park protects an extraordinary mix of marine and terrestrial environments across the central Exuma Cays. Discover quiet shorelines, shallow turquoise waters and the beauty of an area best enjoyed thoughtfully and responsibly.

01 / Protected landscapes

Protected Exuma Cays

Take in dramatic island outlines, natural coastlines and scenic cays surrounded by the brilliant waters of the Exuma chain.

02 / Clear blue water

Crystal-Clear Waters

Admire the remarkable clarity and shifting shades of Bahamian blue, with beautiful views above and below the surface.

03 / Marine habitats

Marine Life

The park’s protected marine environments support fish, coral communities and other wildlife. Observe nature carefully and avoid disturbing animals or habitats.

04 / Quiet shorelines

Remote Beaches & Cays

Enjoy peaceful natural scenery and a slower pace amid the park’s beaches and islands. Access and landing conditions can vary with weather and local rules.

05 / Island exploration

Explore by Boat

A boat journey can reveal the park’s scattered cays and coastal views. Plan your route carefully and check access restrictions before setting out.

Travel thoughtfully

Make room for nature

The Land & Sea Park is a special place because its natural environments are protected. A little preparation helps you enjoy the scenery while helping keep it healthy for future visitors.

Before travelling, check current park guidance and local conditions. Regulations, access, weather and sea conditions may affect where you can go and what activities are permitted.

  • Follow all posted rules and current park guidance.
  • Keep a respectful distance from wildlife.
  • Do not damage coral, vegetation or other natural habitats.
  • Take rubbish with you and leave the area as you found it.
  • Check marine weather and sea conditions before departure.

Respect this protected area

The Exuma Cays Land & Sea Park is a protected environment. Visitors should verify current rules, permitted activities, mooring and access requirements before their trip. Please avoid treating any location as open for unrestricted use.
