Welcome to Exuma

The Exumas are an archipelago of 365 cays and islands in the Bahamas. Divided into Great Exuma, Little Exuma, and the Exuma Cays, this tropical destination is world-famous for sapphire waters, swimming pigs, and private island retreats.
