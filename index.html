Explore Island Destinations

Big Major Cay

Major Epic: Swimming Pigs Beach. World-renowned uninhabited cay where famous swimming pigs paddle out to greet incoming boats along crystal-clear tropical shores.

Explore Major Cay

Compass Cay

Major Epic: Nurse Shark Sanctuary. Famous for its protected harbor marina where visitors can swim, wade, and interact closely with docile, friendly nurse sharks in shallow turquoise water.

Explore Compass Cay

Stocking Island

Major Epic: Chat 'N' Chill & Stingrays. Located off George Town, featuring high sand dunes, beachside conch bars, ocean hiking trails, and tame stingrays gliding along the shoreline.

Explore Stocking Island

Pipe Creek Sandbars

Major Epic: Low-Tide Desert Islands. Pure white sandbars rising out of shallow turquoise waters at low tide, providing miles of isolated sandbars surrounded by endless sea.

Explore Sandbars

Staniel Cay

Major Epic: Central Island Hub. The heart of the northern Exumas with an airstrip, yacht club, local dining, and easy boat charter access to all nearby cays.

Explore Staniel Cay

Exuma Cays Land & Sea Park

Major Epic: Protected Marine Reserve. The world's first land and sea conservation park, spanning 176 square miles of untouched coral reefs, sea turtle sanctuaries, and marine life.

Explore Sea Park

Thunderball Grotto

Major Epic: James Bond Underwater Cave. Iconic underwater cave system featured in classic films, renowned for snorkeling through colorful coral formations and schools of tropical fish.

Explore Grotto
