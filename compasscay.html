Discover the destination

A distinctive island experience in the heart of Exuma.

Compass Cay combines the beauty of the Exuma Cays with a memorable marina setting and opportunities to observe nurse sharks in their natural environment.

Compass Cay is one of the memorable stops in the Exuma Cays, known for its beautiful turquoise waters, natural surroundings, marina and famous nurse sharks. Visitors can explore the island, enjoy the peaceful scenery and experience the clear shallow waters around the cay.

The area is especially popular with visitors arriving by boat. Its marina offers a recognisable stopping point for people exploring this part of the Bahamas, and the island can be included in a wider itinerary of nearby beaches, sandbars and other scenic destinations.

As with any wildlife encounter, visitors should follow local guidance, give animals appropriate space and treat the surrounding marine environment with care. Conditions and access can vary, so it is wise to confirm details with a local operator before setting out.

Plan a rewarding visit

  • ✓Plan your boat trip. Compass Cay is commonly visited as part of an island-hopping itinerary.
  • ✓Pack for the sun. Bring drinking water, sun protection and essentials for time outdoors.
  • ✓Respect marine life. Observe nurse sharks responsibly and follow instructions from local guides.
  • ✓Allow time to explore. Nearby cays and sandbars may make excellent additions when weather permits.

The destination

Compass Cay

Explore the natural beauty of Compass Cay, where island scenery and brilliant sea colours create a peaceful setting. Take in the shoreline, appreciate the surrounding waters and enjoy a slower pace in one of the best-known corners of the Exuma Cays.

Island harbour

Compass Cay Marina

The marina is a familiar stopping point for visitors exploring the surrounding islands by boat. It offers a practical place to pause during a day on the water and serves as a gateway to the distinctive scenery and experiences found throughout this part of Exuma.

Wildlife encounter

Nurse Sharks

Compass Cay is widely associated with nurse sharks that are often seen around the marina and its shallow waters. Observing these fascinating marine animals can be a highlight of a visit. Follow local guidance, avoid disturbing wildlife and keep the experience respectful and safe.

The Exuma blues

Turquoise Waters

The clear sea around Compass Cay displays the luminous blue and turquoise shades that make the Exuma Cays so distinctive. Shallow water, sunlight and pale sandy seabeds create a striking coastal palette, ideal for taking in the scenery and appreciating the beauty of the Bahamian waters.

Explore further

An Island Adventure

Compass Cay can be a rewarding stop on a broader exploration of the Exuma Cays. Depending on your route and the conditions, a day on the water may include nearby islands, beaches and sandbars, giving you a richer sense of the region’s remarkable variety.
