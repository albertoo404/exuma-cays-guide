Discover the destination

A small island with a world-famous welcome.

Big Major Cay is one of the best-known destinations in the Exuma Cays, celebrated for its swimming pigs and the beautiful shoreline that surrounds them.

Visitors typically arrive by boat and spend time near the beach, enjoying the clear shallows, taking photographs and observing the pigs along the shoreline and in the water. The setting is naturally beautiful, with pale sand and vivid shades of blue stretching around the island.

A visit to Big Major Cay can also be part of a wider day exploring the Exuma Cays. Many island-hopping routes include nearby Staniel Cay, sandbars and other scenic stops, making it easy to experience several sides of this remarkable island region.

As with any wildlife encounter, enjoy the pigs respectfully, follow local guidance and avoid feeding them anything unless a qualified local guide confirms it is appropriate.

Make the most of your visit

  • ✓Arrive by boat. Plan your visit as part of a guided excursion or suitable local boat trip.
  • ✓Bring sun protection. Pack water, sunscreen and essentials for time outdoors.
  • ✓Respect the wildlife. Keep a considerate distance and follow local instructions.
  • ✓Explore nearby. Combine Pig Beach with other islands and sandbars when conditions allow.

The destination

Big Major Cay

Discover the natural charm of Big Major Cay, where a sweeping shoreline meets the luminous blues of the Exuma sea. The island’s quiet beauty, soft coastal sands and open seascapes offer a striking introduction to this celebrated corner of the Bahamas. Take a moment to appreciate the scenery and the peaceful rhythm of life among the cays.

A famous encounter

The Swimming Pigs

Meet the remarkable residents whose presence has made Big Major Cay famous around the world. Watching the pigs move along the beach and through the shallows is an unmistakably Exuma experience, bringing a playful character to the island’s extraordinary natural setting. Enjoy the encounter calmly and responsibly, giving the animals space and following local guidance.

Sand & sea

Pig Beach

At Pig Beach, pale sand curves beside clear, gently coloured shallows, creating the classic island scenery visitors come to experience. The meeting of beach and sea gives the shoreline its relaxed, inviting atmosphere and makes it a memorable place to pause, take photographs and enjoy the beauty of the Exuma Cays.

The Exuma blues

Turquoise Waters

The waters surrounding Big Major Cay are celebrated for their vivid shades of turquoise, shifting from pale aquamarine in the shallows to deeper blue farther offshore. These clear seascapes frame the island beautifully and capture the distinctive coastal character of Exuma. Conditions and colours change with the light and weather, making every visit feel a little different.
